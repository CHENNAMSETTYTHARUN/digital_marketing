Marketing basics, explained.

The numbers you will see most often, and how they are calculated.

Cost per lead (CPL)

How much you pay, on average, to get one new lead. Lower is better.

Spend ÷ Leads

Conversion rate

The share of leads that become customers. It shows how good your leads really are.

Conversions ÷ Leads × 100

Return on investment (ROI)

How much you earned compared with what you spent. Positive means the campaign paid off.

(Revenue − Spend) ÷ Spend × 100

Help center.

Answers to common questions.

How do I get started?

Select Get Started, enter your business name, choose what you want to improve, then pick the channels you use. Your dashboard is ready right after.

Which channels can I connect?

You can choose Instagram, Google Ads, Facebook and Email during setup, and change your selection at any time.

What does ROI mean on my dashboard?

ROI compares what you earned from your campaigns with what you spent. A figure of +38% means you earned 38% more than you invested.

How often is my data updated?

Your dashboard and insights are based on your connected campaign data, so they reflect your channels as they report in.

How do I contact the team?

Use the contact details on our About page and we will get back to you.
